## Today
- The main map is src/features/map/BywayMap.tsx (MapLibre, layers in src/features/map/layers.ts). When a road is selected, its line is drawn with the `selected-*` layers, and a flat car icon (symbol layer on the `route-car` GeoJSON source) drives along it (route drawing and drive animation in BywayMap.tsx and src/features/map/routeJourney.ts).
- A "3D" button (src/features/map/ReliefControls.tsx) turns on terrain (src/features/map/terrain.ts), tilts the camera, and, only while 3D is on and a road is selected, swaps the flat car for a three.js car (src/features/map/car3d.ts, a MapLibre custom layer) that follows the `route-car` source.
- The same ReliefControls are used by the road page map (src/components/ui/RouteMapCanvas.tsx), and the strip inset map (src/features/strip/InsetMap.tsx) has its own 3D car.

## What the owner asked
"Make path and vehicle also 3D for general map." Build:
1. **3D car everywhere on the main map:** the selected road's car is the three.js model whether or not 3D terrain is on (seen from above when the map is flat, which is fine), following the drive animation exactly as the 3D car does now. Keep the flat icon only as a fallback if WebGL custom layers fail, and keep marine-highway roads using `buildShip`. Same on the road page map (RouteMapCanvas).
2. **3D road path:** the selected road drawn as a 3D road rather than a flat line: a ribbon with real width and a little thickness (raised edges or a kerb, a darker asphalt top and a pale centre stripe, in Rambleroo's earth-toned inks from src/features/map/style.ts / src/components/art/looks.ts, vintage toy look, flat shading), following the terrain height when 3D terrain is on (sample `map.queryTerrainElevation` along the line, or build it as a custom layer in mercator coordinates with elevation) and lying on the ground when it is off. It must stay legible from the whole-country zoom down to street zoom: at low zooms keep the existing flat styled line and fade to the 3D ribbon from around zoom 9 upward, or scale the ribbon width with zoom so it never becomes a hairline or a fat band. The unselected byways stay as they are.
3. Performance: build the ribbon once per selected road (simplify long roads, e.g. Great River Road), not per frame; dispose it on deselect; respect prefers-reduced-motion as the existing drive does.
